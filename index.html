Brand story

PRISM is the triangle, DRIFTER is the spiral — direction and energy.

The spiral is force with nowhere to go; the triangle is the vessel that gives it a heading.

Stabilizing one inside the other creates a new third form, the consciousness that directs the craft.

A compass needle magnetized toward light and repelled by dark, existing in one and aimed at the other.

Visual Loom

A procedural pattern generator built for Prismdrifter, for producing on-brand textures and graphics without building each one by hand.

Every output derives from a single black-and-white gradient, subdivided and layered by a fixed rule set. Random Look generates a full composition in one click; sliders adjust it layer by layer, or drag on the canvas to drift the layers against each other. Exports as SVG or PNG at multiple sizes.

Use it for background textures, print graphics, social support graphics, and anything else that comes up.
